YT Autopilot

A private, single-owner tool for managing the owner's own YouTube music channels.

What it does

YT Autopilot helps one channel owner run three of their own YouTube channels of instrumental relaxation and driving music. With the owner's permission, granted through Google sign-in, it:

  • uploads videos and Shorts that the owner has reviewed and approved;
  • updates titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and playlists of the owner's own videos;
  • reads channel analytics (views, watch time, subscribers) to prepare reports for the owner.

Nothing is published automatically: before every upload the owner reviews the video, title, description and thumbnail, and confirms that the content complies with the YouTube Community Guidelines. The owner keeps final control over everything published.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any channel other than the owner's own.
