Privacy Policy

YT Autopilot · Last updated: November 22, 2026

YT Autopilot ("the app") is a private tool used by a single owner to manage their own YouTube channels. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and how it can be removed.

Data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app uses YouTube API Services to access:

  • channel and video information of the owner's own channels (titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status, playlists, thumbnails);
  • YouTube Analytics reports of those channels (views, watch time, likes, subscriber changes);
  • the ability to upload and update videos on those channels.

The app does not request or use any other Google account data, such as Gmail, contacts or Drive files.

How the data is used

  • to upload videos the owner has approved and to keep their metadata correct;
  • to produce performance reports for the owner;
  • to make backups of video metadata before any change, so changes can be undone.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens, metadata backups and reports are stored only on the owner's own computers. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train machine learning models. No data is collected from viewers or from any other YouTube user.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page. After access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted together with stored backups and reports within 30 days. Stored API data is also refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent through the contact address shown on the app's Google sign-in screen, or by opening an issue in this site's GitHub repository.
